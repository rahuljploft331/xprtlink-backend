Frequently asked questions

How do I get my Expert profile verified?

Submit a valid government-issued photo ID, and secondary documentation if requested. Verification badges are granted after administrative validation.

When do I receive my payouts?

Net earnings, after the standard 15% platform commission, are sent to your verified bank or payout account on the standard settlement schedule, once identity verification is complete.

How do I report or block an abusive user?

Open the message menu, consultation screen, or expert profile in the app to report content or block the user with one tap. Our compliance team reviews reports within 24 hours.

Can I take payments outside XprtLink?

No. Consultations, communications, and payments that start on XprtLink must stay on the platform. See the Expert Standards Agreement for details.
